// THE EATONS ADVANTAGE

Material Science for Zero Impact

Our Eatons products are made from 100% natural sugarcane bagasse—the fibrous residue left after juice extraction. By repurposing this agricultural waste, we create premium tableware that is completely free from harmful toxins, binders, and chemical coatings.

  • Non-Toxic Manufacturing: No chemicals, bleaching agents, or plastic resins.
  • Soil-to-Soil Cycle: Products return to the earth as organic compost within 90 days.
  • Environmentally Safe: No harmful gases or toxic runoff during the entire production lifecycle.
