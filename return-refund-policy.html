  2. RETURN & REFUND POLICY

We accept returns within 5 days of delivery. Products must be in their original packaging and in the same condition as received.

Contact our customer service team via email - customersupport@esselindia.com Or contact us at +91 9836364000 to obtain a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number. Pack the product securely and include the RMA number and a copy of the original invoice.

Once we receive the returned product, we will inspect it and process a refund within 5-7 business days. Refunds will be issued in the form of credit note.

The refund amount will be the full purchase price of the product, minus any shipping costs incurred.

Yes, custom-ordered or personalized products, products that have been used or damaged, and products that are past their expiration date are non-returnable.

Contact our customer service team within 24 hours of delivery. We will provide a replacement or refund, as applicable.
