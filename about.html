Shaping the Future of Sustainable Tableware

At Eatons, we're driven by a passion for quality, sustainability, and innovation. Our journey began with a simple vision: to provide affordable, high-quality tableware solutions to the food industry.

  1. HOME
  2. ABOUT US
// THE EVOLUTION

From Humble Beginnings to Sustainable Leadership

We started with PS foam tableware, providing essential solutions during our early years. However, sensing the shift in global environmental needs, we took a bold step forward.

"We shifted to eco-friendly bagasse sugarcane tableware, reducing our carbon footprint and pioneering sustainable tableware solutions."

This transition marked the beginning of our commitment to circular economy principles and environmentally responsible manufacturing.

// DIVERSIFICATION

Expanding Horizons, Enhancing Excellence

We've expanded our product range to serve every corner of the food industry.

Aluminum Kitchenware

High-performance solutions tailored for restaurants, cloud kitchens, and passionate home cooks.

Reusable Containers

Innovative food storage solutions designed for durability, safety, and repeated use.

Comprehensive Packaging

Simplifying the packaging process with end-to-end solutions for all your logistics needs.
